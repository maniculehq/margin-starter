---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Make this site yours in three edits."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rename the site">
    Open `docs.json` and change `name` to your product's name. The name shows in the header and the browser tab.

    ```json docs.json
    {
      "name": "Acme API"
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your colors">
    Set `colors.primary` to your brand color. Links, buttons and the active sidebar item use it.

    ```json docs.json
    {
      "colors": {
        "primary": "#0F766E"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewrite this page">
    Replace the text in `index.mdx` with an introduction to your product, then commit and push. Your live site picks up the change on its next deploy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Delete the starter pages you don't need, and remove them from `navigation` in `docs.json` too.
</Tip>
